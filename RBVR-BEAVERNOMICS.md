# Rocket Beaver ($RBVR)
# Beavernomics

## Treasury Allocation Model

Every processed fee follows a fixed allocation model.

## Allocation

30% Reserve

Provides protocol treasury stability.

20% Buyback / Burn

Supports token supply reduction mechanisms.

20% Liquidity Growth

Supports liquidity strengthening.

20% Company / Operations

Supports ongoing protocol operations.

10% Founder Compensation

Founder compensation is controlled through:

- Progressive marginal rates
- USD caps
- Overflow routing


## Founder Protection Rules

Founder compensation cannot exceed configured limits.

When limits are reached:

Excess allocation is redirected according to locked protocol rules.

## Design Principle

The economic system is designed around deterministic execution rather than discretionary intervention.
